    Governance Rules

    The OCI Governance Rules control plane, API version 20220504. A parent tenancy defines rules for the child tenancies of its organization: quotas, tags or the regions children may use. Inclusion criteria pick the children a rule applies to, and a tenancy attachment records the rule applied to one child. Real OCI serves it on governance-rules.organizations.{region}.oci.{domain}; floci-oci serves it on port 4599 with the other services.

    Supported operations

    Area Operations
    Governance rules CreateGovernanceRule, GetGovernanceRule, ListGovernanceRules, UpdateGovernanceRule, DeleteGovernanceRule, RetryGovernanceRule
    Inclusion criteria CreateInclusionCriterion, GetInclusionCriterion, ListInclusionCriteria, DeleteInclusionCriterion
    Tenancy attachments GetTenancyAttachment, ListTenancyAttachments, RetryTenancyAttachment
    Enforced governance rules GetEnforcedGovernanceRule, ListEnforcedGovernanceRules
    Work requests GetWorkRequest, ListWorkRequests, CancelWorkRequest, ListWorkRequestErrors, ListWorkRequestLogs (/20220504/workRequests)

    Wire notes

    • CreateGovernanceRule and CreateInclusionCriterion return 200 with the resource, an etag, an opc-work-request-id and a location header. Update, delete and both retries return 202 with an opc-work-request-id and no body. Every work request is already SUCCEEDED.
    • Lists are wrapped ({"items":[…]}) and paged with opc-next-page. Summaries leave out the template. sortBy takes timeCreated or displayName, sortOrder ASC or DESC.
    • ListGovernanceRules and ListTenancyAttachments need compartmentId or governanceRuleId (400 MissingParameter otherwise), as the SDK documents; ListInclusionCriteria needs governanceRuleId.
    • The template is polymorphic on type: ALLOWED_REGIONS needs displayName and regions (known region names), QUOTA needs displayName and statements, TAG needs name. A template whose type differs from the rule's is 400 InvalidParameter.
    • Rules are created in the root compartment (the tenancy OCID) of a tenancy that is not a child. Another compartment is 400 InvalidParameter.
    • CancelWorkRequest on a finished work request, and changing a DELETED rule or inclusion criterion, are 409 Conflict.
    • GetEnforcedGovernanceRule returns an etag that changes whenever the rule is edited or its tenancy attachment changes.

    Enforcement

    ALLOWED_REGIONS is enforced: a child governed by such a rule cannot subscribe to a region outside its list. CreateRegionSubscription answers 400 InvalidParameter and names the rule. A child is governed when it is an ACTIVE member of the organization, its governanceStatus is OPTED_IN (see AddGovernance and RemoveGovernance in Organizations), and an active inclusion criterion of the rule covers it (ALL, or TENANCY naming it).

    QUOTA and TAG rules are stored, attached and listed as enforced rules, but have no effect: floci-oci has no quotas or tagging service to apply them to.

    Emulator choices

    The SDKs give the shapes but not the behaviour, so floci-oci picks:

    • Data is global, like Organizations: rules, criteria and attachments are kept with the parent tenancy and seen from every region.
    • Tenancy attachments are kept in step with the rules: a child that becomes governed gets an ACTIVE attachment, and one that stops being governed (it opts out, leaves, or the rule or criterion is deleted) has its attachment set to DELETED.
    • Deleting a rule sets it and its inclusion criteria to DELETED; they can still be read.
    • An enforced governance rule is the child's view of one attachment: its compartmentId is the child tenancy, and only the child can read it.
    • OCIDs: ocid1.governancerule…, ocid1.inclusioncriterion…, ocid1.tenancyattachment…, ocid1.enforcedgovernancerule….
    • The corpus does not say how real OCI enforces ALLOWED_REGIONS or which error it returns; refusing the region subscription with 400 InvalidParameter is floci-oci's choice.

    Quickstart

    E="--endpoint http://localhost:4599"
TENANCY=ocid1.tenancy.oc1..flocilocaltenancy0000000000000000000000000000000000000000

oci governance-rules-control-plane governance-rule create-governance-rule-allowed-regions-template $E \
  --compartment-id "$TENANCY" --display-name only-ashburn --type ALLOWED_REGIONS --creation-option TEMPLATE \
  --template-display-name only-ashburn --template-regions '["us-ashburn-1"]'
oci governance-rules-control-plane governance-rule list $E --compartment-id "$TENANCY"
